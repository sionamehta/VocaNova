The 30-second scan

  1. Key: find do from the key signature.
  2. Time: how many beats per measure?
  3. First note: what syllable do you start on?
  4. Rhythm: tap the hardest rhythm once.
  5. Leaps: spot the big jumps and name them.
  6. Accidentals: sharps, flats or naturals?
  7. Patterns: anything repeat?
  8. Ending: it usually ends on do.
